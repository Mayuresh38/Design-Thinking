📋 Design Thinking & SCAMPER Case Study Summary

How the 5 Stages & SCAMPER Produced This Tool:

  • 1. Empathize: 13 in-depth interview findings proved that interns understand their role, but lack day-to-day workflow clarity, hesitate to ask "obvious" questions, experience conflicting advice, and rely on past completed examples as their #1 resource.
  • 2. Define: Scoped problem statement on visibility into workflows, responsibilities, and resources.
  • 3. SCAMPER Ideation:
    • Substitute: Replaced human hesitation with a private AI assistant.
    • Combine: Merged checklist + contacts + examples into one workspace.
    • Adapt: Progressive reveal (Day 1 / Day 3 / Week 2) avoiding info dumps.
    • Modify/Magnify: Elevated completed work into a prominent benchmark gallery.
    • Eliminate: Auto-tagged tasks and contacts with explicit responsibility areas.
    • Reverse: Flipped mentor check-ins so mentors are prompted to reach out first.
  • 4. Prototype: This standalone digital tool ("InternHub") with an added Manager Customizer mode for continuous improvement.
  • 5. Testing: Usability feedback collected directly from friends and peers via the built-in feedback module!

Full academic case study is documented in DESIGN_THINKING_REPORT.md in your workspace.
